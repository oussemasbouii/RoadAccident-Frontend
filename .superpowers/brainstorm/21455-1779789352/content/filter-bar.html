How should the date range filter work?

This controls the time window for all KPI calculations, the time-of-day grid, cause ranking, and period comparison.

A

Preset chips only

Fast one-click switching between fixed windows. No date picker needed — simpler UI, works well for operational monitoring.

Period: Today 7 days 30 days 90 days 1 year ↓ Export
Severity: ● Critical ● High ● Medium ● Low Status: Active Resolved
B

Presets + custom date range picker

Same preset chips, but also a "Custom" option that opens a start/end date picker. Useful for investigations of a specific incident window (e.g., "Ramadan period" or a specific month).

Period: Today 7d 30d 90d 1y 📅 Custom… ↓ Export
📅 From: 2026-04-01 To: 2026-04-30 Apply
Severity: ● Critical ● High ● Medium ● Low

Either way, severity and status are multi-select chips. The date range is the main decision here.
